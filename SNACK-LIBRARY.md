## 1. Snack 1 — sixty light assemblies

Morning slot. Light, largely raw, assembled rather than cooked. **Sixty variants, one
a day, repeating after sixty days** — at that spacing a repeat does not register as
repetition, so the slot stays fresh with no further authoring.

No method is stored. Nobody needs instructions for a cucumber kosumbari. Each entry is
a name and its components.

**Rotation rule:** draw across families, not down them. Seven consecutive sprouts would
feel repetitive even though each is a distinct entry, so the generator should not pick
the same family on consecutive days.

**Season.** Entries marked below are excluded outside their months
(`REQUIREMENTS.md` §4.7). Everything unmarked runs all year. Because exclusions shrink
the pool, the repeat window is *available pool minus one*, not a fixed 59 — the rotation
stays exact over whatever is in season.

## 3. Notes

- **No page numbers here.** These are not from the book, so the tier-1 fallback
  (`REQUIREMENTS.md` §5) does not apply. A Snack 1 entry is complete as it stands.
- **Seasonality is tagged**, not ignored. Six entries carry months; the other 54 run all
  year. The months are from general coastal-Karnataka seasons and want checking against
  what the local market actually does.
- **Sixty is the full library, not the cycle length.** The cycle is however many are in
  season that month — 60 from April to June, 59 in March, 56 in July and 54 from August to
  February (counted from the six tags above). Adding entries lengthens
  the cycle and is always safe.
- **No page numbers, and no season on the Snack 2 additions.** Those are pantry-stable
  (poha, chana, makhana, millet) and need no tag.
